Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MTM Works ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile applications we publish on Google Play (the "Apps").

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other information that directly identifies you through the Apps.

The Apps use third-party services, listed below, that may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, app interactions, and crash or diagnostic data.

2. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (advertising): used to display ads. AdMob may use the advertising ID and other device information to show ads, including personalized ads where permitted, and to measure ad performance. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: see the Google Privacy Policy.

3. How information is used

Information collected by these services is used to provide and improve the Apps, display ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.

4. Your choices

  • Where required by law (for example, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the Apps ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice from the App's settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Children

The Apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their own policies. If you have a request regarding your data, please contact us.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
